# Refined Mockups Questions

Context: the React screens for the contract management system (stories US1.1 to US6.3). Dana, a contract manager, enters contracts, sees the generated schedules, and opens saved contracts.

## Q1. [Designer] How should the contract entry form be laid out?

The form holds contract details, one or more payment streams, and an expense method (stories US1.1, US2.1, US3.1).

A. One page with three sections (Details, Payment streams, Expense), and a Save button at the bottom
B. A three-step wizard (Details, Payment streams, Expense), with a review step before Save
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A
