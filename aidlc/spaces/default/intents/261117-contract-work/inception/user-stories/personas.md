# Personas

## P1. Dana, Contract Manager (primary and only persona)

- **Role:** Records new contracts and checks the money schedules that come from them.
- **Goals:** Enter a contract once and get correct payment and expense schedules without building them by hand in a spreadsheet. Find a saved contract later and read its schedules.
- **Pain points:** Hand-built schedules have date and rounding mistakes. Month-end payment dates are easy to get wrong. Totals that do not add up cost time to trace.
- **Context:** Works in a browser on a desktop. Enters a handful of contracts a week. Each has one to several payment streams. No sign-in in this version, so the system treats every visitor as this persona.

## Persona Relationships and Priority

Only one persona, so no ranking is needed. A finance reviewer (read-only) was considered and is out of scope for this version.
