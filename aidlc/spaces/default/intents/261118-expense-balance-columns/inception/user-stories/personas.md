# Personas

## P1. Dana, Contract Manager (primary and only persona)

- **Role:** Records new contracts and checks the money schedules that come from them. Hands the expense schedule to finance for month-end entries.
- **Goals:** See, for each month, whether the contract is paid ahead of or behind its expense, and how much of that sits on the balance sheet as a short-term or long-term asset or liability, without working it out in a spreadsheet. Read negative amounts the accounting way, in brackets.
- **Pain points:** Working out prepaid and accrued balances by hand is slow and error-prone. Splitting a balance into the part that reverses within 12 months and the rest is easy to get wrong. A minus sign is easy to miss in a long column.
- **Context:** Same as in the existing system: a desktop browser, a handful of contracts a week, no sign-in.

## Persona Relationships and Priority

One persona, so no ranking is needed. A finance reader who uses the balances is still out of scope as a separate user; Dana passes the schedule on.
